:mag: Route tester

Three modes for verifying your alerting pipeline:
  • Simulate — walks Alertmanager's loaded route tree, reports matched receivers. Read-only.
  • Webhook test — POSTs a hardcoded test payload to each receiver's Mattermost webhook. Bypasses Alertmanager. Tests the MM side only.
  • End-to-end — fires a synthetic alert through Alertmanager. Tests the full chain. Real chat posts result.
